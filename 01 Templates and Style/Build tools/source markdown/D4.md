**Warning Signs and Early Intervention**

*The one-page card. Full guidance: Retention, Retrieval and Migration Guide. Issue 1 (Draft), July 2026*

**The signal is a break in his pattern,** not his absolute level of attendance. A Brother who has always come four times a year is not at risk; a Brother who always came and has stopped, is. None of these signs alone is cause for alarm - a combination, or a persistent pattern, warrants action. The two highest-risk windows: the first two years (before and just after Raising) and the year after the Chair.

| **Watch for**     | **Signs a member may be beginning to disengage**                                                                                                                                                             |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Attendance**    | Two or more consecutive meetings missed without explanation • repeatedly arriving late or leaving early • attending only Installations or social events • no longer visiting other Lodges                    |
| **Participation** | Reluctance to accept Lodge work or prepare for office • withdrawal from conversation and social interaction • missing LoI while holding office • distracted, disengaged or unhappy in meetings               |
| **Communication** | Not responding to calls or messages • frustration, disappointment or disillusionment • repeated mentions of money or time pressure • subscription falling into arrears (the Treasurer often sees this first) |

|       | **Step**                               | **What happens**                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1** | **Personal Mentor - informal contact** | Warm, friendly, unforced: a call, a message, a coffee. After any unexplained absence; always within seven days of a second consecutive miss. A welfare call, never a whip - he was missed, and several Brethren asked after him.        |
| **2** | **Lodge Mentor involved**              | If concern continues or the cause needs more than friendship: the Lodge Mentor takes an overview, considers what the Lodge can change, keeps a discreet note, and closes the loop - act, then confirm the member actually feels better. |
| **3** | **Worshipful Master's conversation**   | Where the matter touches Lodge culture, office, or another member: a considered, private conversation - with the Almoner alongside for anything touching welfare.                                                                       |
| **4** | **Provincial referral**                | Where an impartial third party gives the best chance of keeping him - or he may thrive in a different Lodge. Contact the Provincial Grand Mentor's office. Referral is a sign of responsible mentoring, not failure.                    |

**If a resignation letter arrives:** tell the Lodge Mentor, the Almoner and the Secretary the same day. The Secretary acknowledges receipt only - he cannot 'accept' a resignation - and the Lodge has a sixty-day window (Rule 183) in which a resignation can be withdrawn. Roughly one resignation in three is recovered when someone takes the trouble to have the conversation. See the Retention, Retrieval and Migration Guide.

Welfare concerns - health, money, bereavement, family - belong to the Almoner: involve him at once. Relocation? Trigger the migration conversation early - see the Guide.
