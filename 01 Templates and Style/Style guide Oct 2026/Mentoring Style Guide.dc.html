01

The progression idea

The four words of the tagline become four numbered stages. Each has its own colour, stepping from the provincial deep blue at Prepare to the Mentoring teal at Enlighten. The sequence appears as a band in every Word header and footer, as numbered headings in guidance, as stage headers on the pathway slide and along the base of every slide.

04

Shield, bands and footers

Provincial MentoringProvincial Grand Lodge of HertfordshirePrepare – Support – Guide – Enlighten
  • The shield always sits on a white tile inside the blue band, so the official artwork keeps its own colours. Never recolour, crop or redraw it.
  • Clear space: at least one quarter of the shield’s height on every side (shaded above).
  • First page: full-bleed blue identity band with the four-stage strip beneath. Following pages: a slim blue band with the document title and a thin stage strip.
  • Footers: identity and page X of Y (per layout), optional version and review date, and a thin stage strip at the page edge.
  • Bands print to the page edge. Most office printers leave a small white margin, which is expected.
05

Flowcharts

  • Flow runs left to right; branches drop below. Arrows point in the direction of travel.
  • Connectors: 1.75 pt dark blue, arrowhead at the end, attached to connection points.
  • Return routes back into the process: dashed teal.
  • Yes and No labels: 14 pt bold, beside the line just after the decision.
  • All text sits inside its shape; about 25 words per box at most.
  • Placeholder wording is neutral and does not describe an approved provincial procedure.
